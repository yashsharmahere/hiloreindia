How we work

From tech pack to container

Five stages, each with a named owner and a date you can hold us to. No stage starts until the previous one is signed off in writing.

  1. 01

    Tech pack & costing

    You send artwork, measurements and target price. We return a costed BOM with fabric and trim options.

    2–4 days
  2. 02

    Sampling

    Fit sample, then a pre-production sample in the actual fabric. Revised until you sign it off.

    10–14 days
  3. 03

    Bulk production

    Cutting, stitching, and in-house embroidery. Weekly WIP photos so nothing is a surprise at the end.

    30–45 days
  4. 04

    Inline & final QC

    Checks during stitching, then 100% pre-shipment inspection against your approved sample. AQL on request.

    Continuous
  5. 05

    Docs & dispatch

    Cartons, labelling and export documentation prepared to your market's requirements. FOB or CIF.

    5–7 days
