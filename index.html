A note from our founder

From witnessing India's transformation from a closed economy to a global manufacturing hub, I've had the privilege of growing alongside this journey. Over the years, I've worked closely with a wide spectrum of brands, each one shaping my perspective and deepening my commitment to quality.

My foundation lies in the technical heart of garment production across kidswear, womenswear, menswear, and everything in between. I've spent decades refining the process from concept to creation, with a deep focus on construction techniques that deliver precision, consistency, and craftsmanship at scale.

At Hilore India, every stitch carries our promise to honour the trust our clients place in us, and to deliver excellence with integrity.

Deepak Sharma
Founder & CEO, Hilore India
